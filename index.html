Why do your photos look tilted?

We've all been there: you try to take a quick photo of a document or a whiteboard, but because you're standing at an angle, the image looks skewed or "trapezoid" shaped. This is called perspective distortion.

PerspectiveFix uses a smart perspective warp algorithm to pull those corners back into a perfect rectangle. It's like having a high-end document scanner right in your web browser—completely free and without any annoying watermarks.

Perfect for everyday tasks:

  • ✅ Better Document Scans: Fix crooked photos of receipts, ID cards, or contracts.

  • ✅ Architecture & Real Estate: Fix tilted lines in building photos or room interiors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, PerspectiveFix is a 100% free online perspective correction tool. There are no hidden fees, no subscriptions, and we don't add watermarks to your work.

Do I need to upload my private photos to a server?

No. Unlike other "online" tools, our processing happens locally on your device. Your photos are never sent to a server, making it the safest way to fix sensitive documents like contracts, IDs, or personal notes.

Can I use this on my phone?

Absolutely. It's designed to be lightweight and works directly in any modern mobile browser. The touch-friendly corner handles make it easy to fix your "scanned" photos on the go.

What image formats can I use?

You can use JPG, PNG, WebP, and most common image formats. You can also paste an image directly from your clipboard by pressing Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V on Mac).

How does the perspective correction actually work?

When you drag the four corners, the tool calculates a perspective warp transformation. It then "pulls" those corners to form a perfect rectangle, straightening your image mathematically. Think of it like stretching a rubber photo until it looks flat.
